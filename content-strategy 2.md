# Lake Superior Audio Guide - Content Strategy & POI Framework

## Content Philosophy

Every location tells multiple stories. The AI doesn't just deliver facts—it connects geology to Indigenous history, shipwrecks to weather patterns, industry to environment. The guide should feel like traveling with a knowledgeable friend who makes connections you'd never think to ask about.

## Content Depth Levels

### Quick (15-30 seconds)
"You're passing Batchawana Bay—those rounded stones on the beach have been tumbled by waves for thousands of years. The lake never stops polishing them."

### Standard (1-2 minutes)
Covers what it is, why it matters, one interesting connection.

### Deep (3-5 minutes)
Full story with context, connections to other POIs, optional follow-up questions enabled.

### Expert (on-demand)
User asks follow-up questions, AI goes as deep as they want.

## Trigger System

### Distance-Based
- 10km out: Major POI heads-up (optional)
- 2km out: Standard notification
- At location: Full content available
- Passing: Quick mention if not already heard

### Time-Based
- Morning: "Heading north today? Here's what's coming up..."
- Sunset: Light-related content (best viewpoints)
- Dusk: Wildlife viewing, safety reminders

### Contextual
- Weather: Storm content when weather changes
- Speed: Different content for highway vs scenic routes
- Return trip: "Remember that spot? Here's something we didn't mention..."

## Content Production Workflow

1. **Research**: Claude generates draft from reliable sources
2. **Fact-check**: Verify against Parks Canada, Ontario Parks, academic sources
3. **Indigenous review**: All relevant content through partner communities
4. **Audio optimization**: Rewrite for spoken delivery
5. **Multi-depth creation**: Quick/Standard/Deep versions
6. **Recording/TTS**: Generate audio assets
7. **Geolocation mapping**: Assign triggers and zones
8. **QA**: Test in-vehicle on actual route

## Voice & Tone

- **Knowledgeable but not pedantic**: "The Canadian Shield is ancient" not "approximately 2.7 billion years old" (save precision for follow-ups)
- **Curious**: "Have you ever wondered why..." 
- **Connected**: Link stories across categories naturally
- **Respectful**: Especially with Indigenous content—never sensationalize
- **Conversational**: Written to be spoken, not read
